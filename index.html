Confidentiality

BrandDeck respects your right to privacy. Generally, BrandDeck will not make your personal information available to third parties without your consent, subject to this privacy policy.

In addition to our commitment to protecting your privacy, we also recognize the importance of maintaining the confidentiality of any information you provide to us. We will not disclose any confidential information you provide to us, except as required by law or as necessary to provide our services to you.

We only store personal information as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which the personal information was collected, to conduct our business operations and maintain an appropriate historical record of our relationship with you, or in order to comply with applicable laws, whichever is longer, after which time the personal information is destroyed or erased. We may, instead of destroying or erasing your personal information, make it anonymous such that it cannot be associated with, or tracked back to, you.

Personal Information

“Personal Information” is information that can be directly associated with a specific person or entity such as a customer’s name, address, telephone number, e-mail address, credit card number, or information about activities directly linked to that person, such as his or her purchases. Personal Information also includes information that you may choose to provide to us, such as your educational, consumer and employment history profile. We collect Personal Information that you provide to us, such as when you register for BrandDeck, provide BrandDeck with any information or otherwise contact us, when you use any loyalty rBrandDeck or plans that BrandDeck makes available, or when you take any consumer actions with respect to participating vendors (including making purchases or entering the physical location of such vendor).

If you choose to provide Personal Information to us, we will not share this information with third parties or other BrandDeck users without your explicit permission, except when required by law, regulation, or court order or as expressly set forth herein. We may use Personal Information internally – for example, to process your visits and provide you with reliable loyalty plans and rBrandDeck and to tell you about specific goods and services we believe will be of interest to you. BrandDeck will collect this information through the Application to access your location through your mobile device. BrandDeck uses this data for anonymous statistical purposes (see below under “Statistical Information”) If you prefer not to receive promotional information from us, we make it easy for you to let us know. You can contact us at any time to decline promotional information by emailing us at info@myewards.com. We may also contact you periodically in order to provide you with service announcements regarding BrandDeck, but these messages will not contain promotional information. In addition, we use Personal Information to diagnose problems with our servers, to make BrandDeck more useful and to customize BrandDeck and personalize its content for you. Except as anonymised personal information or to Vendors (if you consent), BrandDeck will not share your Personal Information with any third party.
